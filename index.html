Texto completo del artículo
As part of a larger turnaround plan, PepsiCo is cutting 1,000 jobs across North America. The move comes as North American beverage volume fell 2% during the third quarter, while food volume was flat. PepsiCo has tried boosting sales by reducing prices and investing in marketing, as CNBC noted, but it wasn’t working as quickly as hoped. As a result, the beverage giant is now making other decisions to help streamline its operations. When a business is under pressure, jobs are often the first things that leaders cut, Adam Mendler, founder at Adam Mendler Leadership, noted. Mendler, who teaches leadership at UCLA, said that PepsiCo leadership has pointed to a deeper problem with the way the business is organized. “It takes too long to make decisions. If those decisions still need the same approvals after the layoffs, PepsiCo will have fewer people and the same problem.” Allie Russon, director of operations for Salt Aesthetics, feels the layoffs may be a harbinger of things to come. Streamlining operations and flattening the organization can help PepsiCo’s North American units work in closer concert, potentially eliminating inefficiencies, Russon told The Food Institute. But, for those changes to be successful, PepsiCo will have to clarify decision rights and streamline approval processes. And, remaining employees will need to understand which jobs and responsibilities they’re inheriting. The true measure of success will be whether employees can make decisions faster and get more work accomplished. At a time when shoppers are already closely watching what they spend, PepsiCo gave them a reason to switch brands by raising prices. PepsiCo realized its error and has cut prices up to 15%. Yet, the problem is that once shoppers find a cheaper product they like, then PepsiCo needs to win back the customer, which is never easy. “Cutting prices and increasing marketing won’t be enough to win back consumers,” Mendler said. “PepsiCo needs to rethink its strategy from the ground up.” Mendler added that PepsiCo will likely “[analyze] its product offering, the value shoppers perceive in its brands, and its pricing structure to find out what’s stopping customers from picking up its products at the supermarket shelf.” Russon expects 2027 to be PepsiCo’s year to prove its restructuring efforts have meaningfully enhanced its execution capabilities. Savings alone won’t be enough to reverse PepsiCo’s fortunes, particularly if North American volumes remain challenged. Russon said the savings should create an opportunity for PepsiCo to redirect resources toward product and pricing investments and operational capabilities that better align with customers’ needs and demands. “Absent that, PepsiCo’s turnaround efforts could remain under pressure. Volumes and execution post-restructuring should be key items to watch for investors.” “Generations of shoppers grew up with PepsiCo’s brands, and that gives the company a meaningful advantage,” Mendler said. “After the layoffs, employees need to know where PepsiCo plans to invest [next].”

Jack Daniel’s Annual Heroes Selection Honors Our Nation’s Military The 2026 limited release Single Barrel Barrel Proof Tennessee Rye Whiskey was selected by Army veteran and Jack Daniel Distillery foreman Jason Bobo Lynchburg, Tennessee (November 11, 2026) – The Jack Daniel Distillery today announced the limited release of the 2026 Jack Daniel’s Heroes Selection – a Single Barrel Barrel Proof Tennessee Rye Whiskey selected by heroes, for heroes. U.S. Army veteran and Jack Daniel’s employee Jason Bobo was selected to choose this year’s expression. The 2026 release began with Jack Daniel’s classic Tennessee Rye Whiskey mash bill of 70% rye, 18% corn, and 12% malted barley, which was distilled, charcoal mellowed, and then matured in new American white oak barrels on the highest floors of barrelhouses in Lynchburg, Tennessee. The 2026 Heroes Selection was bottled at proofs between 125 and 134, adorned with a military-inspired medallion and packaged in a collectible gift box. Selector Jason Bobo, who served in the U.S. Army for eight years and completed tours in Afghanistan and Iraq, joined Jack Daniel’s in 2011 and currently works as a warehousing foreman at the Lynchburg distillery. Jason’s selection for the annual series features a complex profile that starts with a sweet, soft aroma of apple and molasses, followed by a lingering, spicy mid-palate, finishing with notes of sweet smoke and cherrywood. “The men and women of our military make an enormous sacrifice to protect our freedoms, and it’s a privilege to celebrate them each year with our Heroes Selection,” said Chris Fletcher, Jack Daniel’s Master Distiller. “Veterans like Jason play a critical role crafting our Tennessee Whiskey throughout the year, and he’s made a strong selection for this year’s release that is certainly worthy of his fellow service members.” The annual Heroes Selection release is part of Jack Daniel’s ongoing commitment to those who serve. Since 2011, Jack Daniel’s Operation Ride Home campaign, in partnership with the Armed Services YMCA, has raised over $4.65 million to help more than 16,000 junior-enlisted service members and those with families travel home for the holidays. The 2026 Jack Daniel’s Heroes Selection Single Barrel Barrel Proof Tennessee Rye Whiskey (750ml) is available now for a suggested retail price of $69.99 at select military retail locations and in limited quantities at the White Rabbit Bottle Shop at the Jack Daniel Distillery. Officially registered by the U.S. Government in 1866 and based in Lynchburg, Tenn., the Jack Daniel Distillery is the first registered distillery in the United States and is on the National Register of Historic Places. Jack Daniel’s is the maker of the world-famous Jack Daniel’s Old No. 7 Tennessee Whiskey, Gentleman Jack Double Mellowed Tennessee Whiskey, Jack Daniel’s Single Barrel Tennessee Whiskey, Jack Daniel’s Tennessee Honey, Jack Daniel’s Tennessee Fire, Jack Daniel’s Tennessee Apple, Jack Daniel’s Tennessee Blackberry, Jack Daniel’s Bonded, Jack Daniel’s Sinatra Select and Jack Daniel’s & Coca-Cola. Today, Jack Daniel’s is a true global icon found in more than 170 countries around the world and is the most valuable spirits brand in the world as recognized by Interbrand.
