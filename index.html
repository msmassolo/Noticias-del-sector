Beer52 chief revenue officer Elliot Fisher: Eebria has a ‘much bigger opportunity ahead of us’ Drinks distributor Eebria has relaunched with a new brand and website overhaul, following its pre-pack sale to Beer52 in 2024. The B2B platform has been revamped following investment across its technology , commercial proposition and team. Founded as an online marketplace connecting producers directly with the licensed trade, it now seeks to become an “important national sales channel for drinks producers”. Beyond simply allowing suppliers to list products on its website, Eebria was now developing “structured growth partnerships with producers”, combining trade distribution with marketing activity, helping with new customer acquisition and providing opportunities for suppliers across the wider Beer52 business. “The ambition is to make Eebria much more than somewhere producers list their products and wait for orders to arrive,” said Jordan Wallace, head of business development at Eebria. “We’re increasingly sitting down with producers and asking what growth looks like for them, which customers they want to reach and what we can do together to make that happen. Equally, we’re working closely with pub groups and independent operators to help them discover brilliant products they wouldn’t necessarily find through traditional wholesale . “The new brand and platform better reflect the business we’re becoming. There’s a huge opportunity to make Eebria one of the most effective ways for ambitious drinks producers to build a genuinely national trade presence.” To support the next stage of its growth, Beer52 chief revenue officer Elliot Fisher has been appointed as Eebria’s managing director. Fisher had “worked closely with the Eebria team since the business was acquired by Beer52 in 2024”, Beer52 said. “Eebria is a fantastic business with a genuinely valuable role to play in the UK drinks industry,” Fisher said. “It gives producers a route to thousands of trade customers without many of the traditional barriers associated with national distribution, while giving operators access to an incredible breadth of products in one place. “We’ve invested significantly in the platform, the brand and the team because we believe there is a much bigger opportunity ahead of us. The new website is the most visible part of that investment, but behind it we’re building a stronger commercial business and developing much closer partnerships with both producers and customers. “My focus is now on building on those foundations and making Eebria an increasingly important route to market for drinks producers across the UK. That means growing our network of trade customers, developing deeper partnerships with producers and using the wider reach of Beer52 to create opportunities that neither business could deliver in isolation.” Eebria collapsed into administration in March 2024, before certain assets of the business were acquired by online beer retailer Beer52 for just £30k. LOS ANGELES, Calif. — August 21, 2026 — Asterisk Imports today announced its official launch, securing the exclusive U.S. import and distribution rights to the Kasteel portfolio from Kasteel USA, the self-owned U.S. import company of Belgium's Brouwerij Vanhonsebrouck. The deal marks the start of a multi-year initiative to establish Kasteel as one of the leading Belgian specialty beer brands in the United States. The rights acquired by Asterisk Imports cover the complete Kasteel lineup, including award-winning specialty ales, fruit beers, non-alcoholic offerings, and new product innovations. The partnership is backed by pricing commitments through 2029, giving distributors and retailers pricing stability and a solid foundation for long-term brand growth. "Kasteel is already one of Belgium's largest specialty breweries. We believe its greatest opportunity still lies ahead in the United States," said James Curti, Founder and CEO of Asterisk Imports. Our goal is simple: make Belgian beer more accessible to American consumers without compromising on quality." "We believe Asterisk Imports has the passion, experience, and vision to help Kasteel reach its full potential in the U.S. market," said Xavier Vanhonsebrouck. Strategic Beverage Services has acquired the US operations of beverage processing specialist BevZero, creating what the companies describe as the largest dealcoholisation service provider in the United States. The Santa Rosa, California-based Strategic Beverage Services said the acquisition will expand its capacity and geographic reach as demand for low- and no-alcohol beverages continues to grow across the US market. The combined business will serve wineries, distilleries, cider producers and functional beverage companies, bringing together Strategic Beverage Services' existing operations with BevZero-US's dealcoholisation technology, technical expertise and industry experience. Patrick Morgan, chief operating officer of Strategic Beverage Services, said the acquisition would strengthen the company's ability to provide dealcoholisation services at scale. The deal also positions the combined business to compete in what Morgan described as the fastest-growing segment of the beverage industry. BevZero-US is headquartered in California and employs winemakers, distillers and food scientists supporting beverage producers from product development through to commercial production. Its services include regulatory guidance, equipment and technical expertise, alongside processing capabilities designed to help producers develop low- and no-alcohol products. The company has built its reputation around dealcoholisation, using processes designed to remove alcohol while retaining the aroma and flavour characteristics of the original beverage. The acquisition comes as beverage producers across wine, spirits and cider increasingly explore alcohol-free and reduced-alcohol formats in response to changing consumer preferences. The transaction follows a strong showing for BevZero-US and its customers at the third annual International Non-Alcoholic Competition in New Jersey in June 2026. Alongside dealcoholisation, the combined business offers a range of beverage processing services, including co-packing and production, alcohol adjustments, concentration and cross-flow filtration. It also provides high-proof spirits for sanitation or blend adjustment, SO2 removal, stuck fermentation and volatile acidity removal, as well as wine destruction and disposal. BevZero has been a specialist in alcohol removal since pioneering vacuum distillation for dealcoholization in 1991. The business has since expanded into low- and no-alcohol and functional beverage processing across wine, cider and spirits. Strategic Beverage Services said the combination with BevZero-US will give producers access to greater processing capacity and a broader range of services as brands seek to develop and scale products for the expanding alcohol-free and reduced-alcohol market. New figures from American analytics and advisory firm Gallup detail that US drinking levels remains at record lows. In 2026, the proportion of Americans who consume alcohol remains at 54%, the same figure as last year. This represents a steep decline from 2022 when 67% of survey respondents said they drank alcoholic beverages. The data for this figure comes from its ‘Consumption Habits’ survey based on interviews with US adults aged 18 and older which took place from 1 to 19 July 2026. Gallup’s data stretches back to 1939, with the highest level of alcohol consumption being 71%, from the period 1976 to 1978.
