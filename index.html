As a result, the beverage giant is now making other decisions to help streamline its operations. Mendler, who teaches leadership at UCLA, said that PepsiCo leadership has pointed to a deeper problem with the way the business is organized. “It takes too long to make decisions. But, for those changes to be successful, PepsiCo will have to clarify decision rights and streamline approval processes. And, remaining employees will need to understand which jobs and responsibilities they’re inheriting. The true measure of success will be whether employees can make decisions faster and get more work accomplished. At a time when shoppers are already closely watching what they spend, PepsiCo gave them a reason to switch brands by raising prices. PepsiCo realized its error and has cut prices up to 15%. Savings alone won’t be enough to reverse PepsiCo’s fortunes, particularly if North American volumes remain challenged.
