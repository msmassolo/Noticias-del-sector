Wearin said, the challenge in the brewery was ensuring the final product still delivered on flavour. “Getting all that body, richness and mouthfeel you expect from a stout without the alcohol doing the heavy lifting is a clever art, particularly when you’re also keeping the sugar at zero and the calories down. “Our Head Brewer, Ian Jones, has done an amazing job with this beer,” she said. Control of the brewing and fermentation process then keeps the alcohol, sugar and calories down. La cerveza mantuvo su tendencia a la baja en las cuatro semanas cerradas el pasado 1 de agosto en EEUU, con una caída del 3,9% en ventas en valor y del 5,2% en volumen, según el seguimiento de NielsenIQ sobre el canal minorista estadounidense.
