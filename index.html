Texto completo del artículo
Rory Gammell, founder of whisky brand Wee Smoky, has partnered with bartender and drinks consultant Jack Sotti to create a line of ready-to-drink (RTD) cocktails. Wee Smoky is a single grain Scotch whisky finished in peated casks, which Gammell created specifically for cocktails in 2020 . While promoting the brand, Gammell realised many consumers believe they don’t like whisky and that cocktails can introduce a new audience to the category. Sotti, who won Diageo World Class Australia in 2015 and now heads up London’s Archive & Myth, worked as the brand’s liquid development partner and created six classic cocktails. “Wee Smoky takes a flavour that has traditionally been viewed as complex or intimidating and makes it approachable and exciting,” said Sotti. “The goal was never simply to put whisky into a can – it was to create properly balanced cocktails that deliver the flavour and experience you would expect from a great bar serve.” The range includes two traditional whisky cocktails – the Old Fashioned and the Manhattan – as well as branching out to non-whisky serves: the Margarita, the Espresso Martini and the Grape-fruit-arita, a riff on the Paloma. The collection is finished with the Salted Caramel cocktail, a unique creation for the brand, described as ‘dessert, tailored for your pocket’. Gammell added: “In consumer testing, so many people who swore they wouldn’t drink whisky really enjoyed our cocktails, saying they would order them at a bar. There is so much opportunity here because instead of turning away from smoke, people are excited about it. “Smoke has traditionally been seen as intense. Whisky has traditionally been seen as intimidating. We believe both deserve a wider audience. “Imagine finding out that your favourite Margarita, or indeed Paloma, turned out to be Scotch whisky-based. For me that’s what success would look like – playing our part in a broader perception shift of our category.” Wee Smoky claims the RTD range is the ‘first’ of its kind dedicated to smoky whisky. Each cocktail comes in a 100ml can, with ABVs ranging from 17% for the Grape-fruit-arita to 32.1% for the Old Fashioned. The range is available in the UK from the brand’s website, with distribution secured with Select Drams. A mixed six-pack costs £30 (US$40), while four packs of each expression cost between £20 and £25 (US$27-US$33). The brand has plans to launch in North America and Australia. Wee Smoky has previously worked with Bemakers to promote its range in six European markets .
