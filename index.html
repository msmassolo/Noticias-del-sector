Beverage entrepreneur Lance Collins has launched ZenWTR, a premium bottled water brand combining alkaline hydration with packaging made from recycled, certified ocean-bound plastic. Collins, who previously founded beverage brands including FUZE, NOS, BODYARMOR and CORE Hydration, is positioning ZenWTR as a challenger in the premium water category, with packaging and environmental impact playing a central role in the brand proposition. The water is vapour distilled before undergoing ionisation through electrolysis to reach a pH of 9.5. Calcium, potassium and magnesium are then added as part of a proprietary mineral and electrolyte blend intended to deliver what the company describes as a crisp, clean taste. “The future of bottled water should be defined by both what is inside the bottle and the impact that bottle can have beyond itself,” said Collins, founder and CEO of ZenWTR. ZenWTR's flagship 20oz bottle is made from 100% recycled, certified ocean-bound plastic collected from coastal areas where recycling infrastructure is limited. According to the company, its supply network has sourced material from Indonesia, Thailand, Ecuador and Paraguay. Collection partners work with local communities to develop collection infrastructure and bottle-deposit programmes, while providing income opportunities for collectors. The company is making the recycled material part of its product story rather than treating packaging as a secondary sustainability initiative. A preform supplied as part of ZenWTR's media launch materials demonstrates how the recovered plastic is heated and blown into the finished bottle. ZenWTR also says it supports programmes that recover and recycle the equivalent of five ocean-bound plastic bottles for every qualifying Alumi-Tek bottle produced. The brand has additionally committed 1% of ZenWTR revenue to ocean conservation and recycling education, advancement and access in the US. ZenWTR says it became the first beverage brand to receive Plastic Negative Certification following a third-party assessment of its plastic footprint. Through a partnership with the Plastic Credit Exchange, the company says it supports the recovery and recycling of plastic waste equivalent to 200% of its annual plastic footprint. The certification and plastic recovery programme form a significant part of ZenWTR's positioning as it enters a bottled water market increasingly shaped by questions around packaging waste and environmental impact. “We want to prove that consumers do not have to compromise on quality or taste to support meaningful environmental impact,” Collins said. “Every bottle is an invitation to rethink what an everyday purchase can accomplish.” ZenWTR is currently available through retailers including Whole Foods Market, QuikTrip, Walmart, Amazon, Circle K's Frontier Division, Kwik Trip, Bristol Farms and Erewhon. New York, NY - Iconic global Italian wine powerhouse Cantine Riunite announces the launch of 187ml bottles of Riunite Zero Red Semi-Sparkling and Maschio Zero White Sparkling, bringing two of its alcohol-free expressions to a portable, single-serve format. Debuting in September, the new bottles will begin rolling out at U.S. retailers in October. The launch arrives as non-alcoholic beverages continue to gain traction and shoppers increasingly embrace smaller sizes that offer greater flexibility for evolving drinking occasions. These shifts are unfolding alongside continued growth in convenience retail, creating a timely opportunity for Riunite Zero and Maschio Zero to reach consumers beyond traditional wine-purchasing locations. The new formats also build on strong momentum for both the category and the brands. The U.S. alcohol-free category surpassed $1 billion in off-premise retail sales by the end of 2025, according to NielsenIQ, while online non-alcoholic beverage sales surged approximately 208% year over year, according to Pinky Beverages’ 2026 NA Trends Analysis. Riunite Zero and Maschio Zero have mirrored that growing demand internationally, with worldwide sales across 2024 and 2025 reaching 200,000 bottles of Riunite Zero and 150,000 bottles of Maschio Zero, respectively, the majority of which were sold in 2025. Together, the acceleration reflects increasing global enthusiasm for the brands’ zero-proof portfolio and sets the stage for continued expansion in the U.S. “Today’s consumers are looking for choices that fit more seamlessly into their lifestyles, and these new 187ml bottles give them another way to enjoy Riunite Zero and Maschio Zero on their own terms,” said Mina Belhaj Rouas, Vice President of Marketing and Trade Development at Frederick Wildman & Sons, the exclusive U.S. importer of Riunite and Maschio. “Whether it’s about moderation, portability or simply enjoying one serving at a time, this size makes it easier to choose an alcohol-free sparkling wine wherever the moment takes you.” Designed for everything from casual gatherings and picnics to travel and spontaneous celebrations, the individual size makes both wines easier to enjoy without committing to a full-size bottle. The compact size also creates new possibilities in convenience and grab-and-go retail settings, bringing the brands into spaces where traditional wine bottles may be less practical. Suggested retail pricing is $2.99 for Riunite Zero 187ml and $3.99 for Maschio Zero 187ml. For more information and purchase details as the new offerings become available, please visit Riunite website. Riunite, established in 1950, is an Italian wine brand rooted in collaboration and excellence. Originating from a coalition of 9 co-op wineries in the Province of Reggio Emilia, Riunite has grown into a global leader in grape and wine production. With over 1,450 families of winegrowers, 8 cooperative winemaking centers, and 4,600 hectares of vineyards, Riunite processes 89,000 tons of grapes annually. Committed to sustainability and quality, Riunite portfolio represents a diverse range of varietals, including its renowned Lambrusco. Produced without added sugars, Riunite wines offer an approachable character and natural sweetness. In every bottle, Riunite invites you to savor the essence of Italian craftsmanship. Maschio, founded in 1973, is a benchmark in Italian sparkling wine, rooted in the heart of the Veneto region between the Dolomites and Venice. As a pioneer in the production of Prosecco DOC, Maschio helped define the fresh, fruit-forward style that has made Prosecco one of Italy’s most beloved exports. With a focus on native varietals and a commitment to innovation, Maschio blends tradition with modern winemaking techniques to produce bright, expressive wines that reflect the character of northeastern Italy. Today, Maschio continues to lead in quality and volume, offering a portfolio that brings Italian sparkling wine to tables around the world. Finanzas

Coca-Cola capturó la atención inversora con su compromiso de USD 10.000 millones en infraestructura estadounidense hasta 2030, respaldado por un análisis positivo de UBS sobre visibilidad de dividendos y ganancias. En el lado negativo, National Beverage Corp tocó mínimos de 12 meses tras reportar ingresos de USD 330,7 millones en Q1 —un 3,3% por debajo de estimaciones— y una caída del 16% en ganancias netas, mientras Diageo y Molson Coors también enfrentaron revisiones a la baja de precio objetivo por parte de bancos de inversión.

Marketing

En contrapartida, Red Bull apuesta a la innovación estacional con el lanzamiento de la Winter Edition Pistachio & Berries para sostener ventas en temporada baja, y Mahou San Miguel desplegó una campaña integrada para toda su gama sin alcohol, reforzando el segmento de mayor crecimiento del sector.

Supply Chain

Diageo enfrenta una crisis operativa en su destilería Cameronbridge (Escocia), donde los trabajadores votaron a favor de huelga tras el anuncio de reducción de producción de whisky escocés, con riesgo real de desabastecimiento en el mercado británico. A nivel local, las exportaciones de vino de Mendoza registraron una caída del 36% en 2026, señalizando pérdida de competitividad internacional que combina factores cambiarios, logísticos y de demanda global.
