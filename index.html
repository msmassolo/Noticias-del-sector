Constellation Acquires SpikedAde for $75 Million as Boozy Sports Drink Segment Booms

Constellation Brands adquirió SpikedAde por USD 75 millones más incentivos de desempeño de hasta USD 278 millones en cinco años, marcando su entrada al segmento de bebidas deportivas alcohólicas (hard sports drinks). Esta categoría está experimentando crecimiento acelerado y representa una oportunidad estratégica para diversificar el portafolio más allá de cerveza tradicional. La operación refleja la tendencia de consolidación en RTD y la apuesta de players globales por segmentos de mayor crecimiento en bebidas funcionales con alcohol.

Leer nota original Ver en español También en: BevNET
Texto completo del artículo
On Tuesday Constellation Brands announced its acquisition of SpikedAde , one of the leading ready-to-drink (RTD) beverages in the growing boozy sports drink segment. Constellation agreed to a payment of $75 million for 100 percent ownership of the fast-growing brand, though payments in the next five years could reach up to an additional $278 million contingent on performance. The acquisition marks Constellation Brands’s first foray into hard sports drinks — a growing segment within the already expanding category of spirit-based ready-to-drink beverages. SpikedAde is the runner-up to “Ade” segment leader Super Lyte, which boasts 65 percent share of off-premise sales, according to Brewbound . Chief executive officer Jason Cohen founded SpikedAde in New Jersey in 2025. It packages non-carbonated, vodka-based drinks with Gatorade-like flavors, such as Electric Orange and Electric Blue, into 12-ounce cans. Cohen claimed he raised $10 million in the first two weeks of this year alone to expand the brand outside of New Jersey. Now, SpikedAde is ranked among the top 25 RTD brands, according to NielsenIQ data. And though SpikeAde’s presence now extends along the East Coast, Constellation’s pickup will bring the brand to markets nationwide. “With Constellation’s scale and expertise behind it, we’re excited to see SpikedAde reach even more consumers with its distinctive positioning and continue its remarkable growth trajectory,” Cohen states in a press release issued by Constellation. The “Ade” category has exploded this past year. With Super Lyte, SpikedAde, and Vodkade leading the charge, the segment burst past the one million case mark this year, according to NielsenIQ data. With better-for-you and health-conscious trends swarming the market, new and established alcohol brands are leaning into sports marketing and functional ingredients like protein and electrolytes . And SpikedAde fits the bill, touting a zero-sugar, 100-calorie liquid. “As consumer preferences evolve and new occasions continue to emerge, we’re excited to build on the strong foundation the SpikedAde team has created,” Constellation president and CEO Nicholas Fink says in the release. The Coca‑Cola Co. will preview portfolio innovations to help refresh, hydrate and recharge throughout the day at the 2026 NACS Show in Las Vegas, Oct. 7-9. What people reach for to drink can look very different from one moment to the next. Launched nationally in June, it’s BODYARMOR’s first sparkling sports drink in a can and comes in five fruit-forward flavors. • vitaminwater zero sugar formula 100 launches in March 2027 in grape, delivering 100% of the daily value of vitamins C, E, B3, B5, B6 and B12. That momentum is creating room to expand the portfolio. Core Power protein shakes, also growing at a double-digit rate, will introduce Core Power Banana in January 2027 in both 26 gram and 42 gram protein offerings, the brand’s first new flavor in almost a decade. Core Power Strawberry will also join the 26 gram lineup. The latest innovations reflect how Coca‑Cola continues to evolve its portfolio around changing consumer preferences. Texto completo del artículo
Nestlé has teamed up with none other than the NBA (National Basketball Association) to make Milo, Nescau and Nesquik Official Partners. The multi‑year deal spans 21 markets across four continents, making it Nestlé’s biggest ever international brand collab to date. The venture, says the Swiss multinational, is designed to engage fans and expand basketball participation around the world. It also reflects Nestlé’s strategy to “focus on fewer, larger global marketing platforms that can be activated consistently across markets, ensuring local relevance”. It’s also part of a broader effort to engage younger consumers through shared interests and experiences that extend beyond traditional advertising. Brands including Milo, Nescau and Nesquik already have strong links to physical activity and youth engagement, making basketball a natural fit as the food and beverage giant seeks to strengthen brand relevance and drive consumer engagement across key markets. As part of the collaboration, Nestlé and the NBA will build on a shared commitment to youth participation in sport by creating more opportunities for young people and families to engage with basketball. This includes activations across select NBA youth basketball development programmes and platforms. “For decades, our brands have believed in the power of sport to bring out the best in people,” says Liberato Milo, SVP head of Confectionery & Snacking Strategic Business Unit at Nestlé. “From Milo and Nescau sports programmes to Nesquik athlete partnerships and initiatives, we have seen how sport can energise people, build confidence and create healthy habits. Together with the NBA, we will use the power of basketball to connect with consumers at scale, create locally relevant experiences and inspire more young people and families to get active and enjoy the game.” Basketball’s growing international reach is also likely to have played a key role in the partnership. With the NBA continuing to expand its fan base outside North America, the sport offers Nestlé an opportunity to engage consumers across multiple regions through a single global platform while tailoring campaigns to local markets. The partnership will include retail programmes and in-market fan experiences, including on-pack promotions featuring NBA branding and opportunities for consumers to win NBA tickets, merchandise and other prizes. “Nestlé is one of the world’s most recognisable brands and has a longstanding connection to consumers across the globe,” says Julie Morris, NBA SVP of commercial development and media. “We look forward to leveraging Nestlé’s reach and local market presence to connect with fans in new ways and support the continued growth of basketball around the world.” The agreement reflects a broader trend within food and beverage, with major brands increasingly turning to global sports partnerships to cut through a crowded media landscape and build stronger emotional connections with consumers. By aligning with major sporting organisations, companies can extend their reach beyond traditional advertising and create immersive experiences that resonate with younger audiences. For Nestlé, the real win will be turning basketball fandom into long-term consumer loyalty. The agreement comes into effect on 1 January 2027.

Lo más importante de los últimos días Ver resumen del período · 8 días · Actualizado 2026-10-02

La semana estuvo marcada por la contracción del consumo en múltiples mercados: Argentina registró caídas consecutivas en supermercados y autoservicios, Europa redujo el gasto en alcohol, y México mostró señales mixtas con formatos de bajo precio como Tiendas 3B ganando terreno frente a operadores tradicionales. El segmento de espirituosos premium atraviesa una corrección severa, con el tequila liderando las caídas en EE.UU. y vinos europeos perdiendo mercados de exportación clave. En contrapartida, los RTDs a base de spirits crecieron 21% en EE.UU. y las bebidas energizantes consolidaron su expansión global, aunque enfrentan creciente presión regulatoria en India. Los movimientos de M&A y reorganizaciones ejecutivas en grandes compañías como Coca-Cola, Diageo y Ambev anticipan una reconfiguración estratégica para 2027.

Top 5 eventos del período

  • El tequila premium colapsa en EE.UU.: Don Julio y Casamigos de Diageo registran caídas drásticas de ventas, poniendo en jaque la apuesta estratégica del sector por el segmento ultra-premium.
  • PepsiCo, Monster y Red Bull demandan al regulador indio FSSAI por la prohibición de usar la etiqueta 'energy drink', en una batalla legal con implicancias globales para la categoría.
  • Coca-Cola ficha a Rob Gehring, ex CEO de Monster Energy para las Américas, como presidente de operaciones para EE.UU. y Canadá, en un movimiento que fortalece su flanco en bebidas energizantes.
  • Los RTDs a base de spirits crecen 21% en EE.UU. según NIQ, con Super Lyte de Stateside Brands proyectando 4 millones de cajas en su primer año y consolidándose como el lanzamiento más exitoso de la categoría.
  • Argentina: caída del consumo en supermercados y mayoristas por séptimo mes consecutivo, con el sector de cerveza artesanal en crisis y el consumidor migrando hacia formatos y marcas más económicas.
Finanzas

Constellation Brands se prepara para reportar resultados de Q2 bajo expectativa de superar estimaciones, mientras Diageo enfrenta la caída simultánea de sus marcas de tequila premium Don Julio y Casamigos en EE.UU. y una huelga en su principal destilería europea, agravando las presiones sobre su valuación. William Grant & Sons reportó una baja del 13% en ganancias y Marie Brizard acumuló una caída de ingresos del 4,4% en H1, reflejando el deterioro generalizado del segmento de espirituosos a nivel global.

Marketing

Coca-Cola activó una edición limitada Holiday Creamy Vanilla para la temporada festiva y ejecutó su estrategia global de packaging vinculada al Mundial FIFA 2026, mientras Diageo duplicó la inversión en White Horse en Brasil apuntando a consumidores jóvenes urbanos. PepsiCo y Red Bull escalan su batalla regulatoria en India contra la prohibición de etiquetar productos como 'energy drinks', una disputa con impacto directo sobre posicionamiento de marca y proyecciones de ingresos en uno de los mercados de mayor crecimiento global.

Supply Chain

AG Barr reportó pérdidas de £10 millones en ventas por problemas de cadena de suministro en H1, aunque mantuvo su guía anual tras resolver las disrupciones; Arca Continental completó una expansión de USD 42 millones en su planta de San Antonio para ampliar capacidad de producción Coca-Cola en Norteamérica. En el plano local, Coca-Cola Argentina lanzó Powerade Zero Mountain Blast, señal de adaptación del portafolio al contexto de sensibilidad de precio del consumidor argentino.

Ventas / Comercial

En Argentina, las ventas de supermercados y autoservicios acumulan siete meses consecutivos de caída con una baja del 2,1% interanual en julio, mientras el e-commerce de consumo masivo representa apenas el 6,7% de las ventas totales, limitando el potencial de compensación digital. A nivel regional, Backus Perú proyecta duplicar su facturación hacia 2028 via expansión de canales, y Ambev utiliza Zé Delivery como laboratorio de consumo en grandes eventos para testear innovaciones y defender participación en canales directos.
